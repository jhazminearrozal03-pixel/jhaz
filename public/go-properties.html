Frequently Asked Questions

Planning your stay

Where are the Go Properties vacation homes located?

Shamrock by the Sea and The Salt Box are located in Wells, Maine, giving guests access to Southern Maine's beaches, restaurants and nearby coastal communities.

How do I check availability?

Use any Check Availability or Book Direct button on this website. You'll be taken to our DirectStays booking site where current availability can be viewed.

Does this website process reservations?

Reservations, availability and payments are handled through our existing DirectStays booking system.

Is The Salt Box pet friendly?

The Salt Box currently allows one dog. Please review the complete property rules before booking.

What time is check-in and check-out at The Salt Box?

The Salt Box currently lists check-in beginning at 4:00 PM and check-out before 11:00 AM.
